Personal branding.

Building trust and building your own pipeline via social media.

Four habits that turn a profile into a lead source — then four content styles to carry them.

SCROLL
01

Pick your niche.

Pick a community of investors or sellers you want to target — and become their go-to expert.

One audience, owned properly, beats ten covered thinly. When they think of their next move, they should think of you first.

02

Stay on trend.

Know which hooks and topics will stop the scroll.

Watch what is working this week — formats, sounds, openers — and move while it is still moving. The first three seconds decide everything.

03

Stay consistent.

It's a flywheel.

The more you post, the more you're seen. The more you're seen, the more you're trusted. The more you're trusted, the more your pipeline fills on its own.

04

Value exchange.

This is about leads — feeding your pipeline with potential deals and clients.

Use the internal tools you already have — guides, reports, market data — to give your audience a reason to reach out to you. Ending videos with a CTA is important.

Styles

The yapper.

Straight to camera. Personality first.

Styles

Data driven.

Let the numbers make the argument.

Transactions, yields, price per square foot, off-plan versus ready. Investors follow the broker who reads the market out loud — a sharp take on this week's numbers positions you as the analyst, not the salesman.

Styles

The educator.

Answer the questions buyers are already asking.

Golden Visa rules, payment plans, service charges, handover checklists, where the new metro line lands. Evergreen, searchable, endlessly shareable — and it pairs naturally with your guides and reports as the CTA.

Styles

Day in the life.

Show the work behind the deals.

Viewings, valuations, the 7am call, the handover keys. Familiarity builds trust faster than any listing — people reach out to the broker they feel they already know.

These styles are suggestions — you're encouraged to implement any of them when and where you see fit.
